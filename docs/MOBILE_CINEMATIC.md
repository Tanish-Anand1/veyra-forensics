# Mobile cinematic

17 October 2026. The mobile experience now uses the same original machined sculpture and seven-second GSAP choreography as desktop when WebGL is available. Phone width alone no longer disables the scene.

## Composition and motion

The portrait camera begins close to the metallic surfaces, opens the fracture, passes through the connected geometry and settles above the login headline. The wordmark reveal remains readable on a quiet dark field. The final pose and login use the same mounted canvas, without a scene swap or a layout resize. A short camera settling movement ends after ten seconds; touch adds a small camera response without intercepting scrolling or provider buttons. Background-page settling motion pauses.

Phone rendering is capped at device pixel ratio 1 and uses demand rendering. Desktop resolution and composition are preserved. Geometry, material textures and lighting are shared; there is no new 3D dependency or remote asset request. A Replay opening control is available on login, and Skip intro remains immediate. Reduced-motion preferences hide replay and use the still composition.

Reduced motion, data saving, unavailable/software graphics and context loss use the matching original portrait poster. Switching to reduced motion also removes a mounted canvas. The mobile poster is preloaded at phone widths. Direct login keeps its provider controls available during graphics preparation.
