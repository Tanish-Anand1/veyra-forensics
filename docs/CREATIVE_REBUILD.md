# Creative rebuild: the intervention chamber

Outcome: make the opening, conversation replay, graph selection and protective action feel like one evidence-led instrument. Preserve the existing local and optional OpenAI analysis paths.

## Live reference study, 16 October 2026

All six supplied sites were opened in a real browser. These are observed compositional choices, not claims about their underlying implementation.

- [Lusion](https://lusion.co/): large cropped mechanical forms, substantial highlights and occlusion, restrained framing. Apply tangible metal and a deliberate camera, with original geometry.
- [Active Theory](https://v5.activetheory.net/): a spatial scene grounds content in an environment; quiet navigation sits at its edges. Apply depth and camera continuity, without reproducing the scene.
- [Unseen 2025](https://2025.unseen.co/): radically scaled compressed lettering, fine italic contrast, and small editorial annotations create distinct reading registers. Apply an original typographic composition without its ASCII treatment or yellow palette.
- [Bruno Simon](https://bruno-simon.com/): a clear start action leads into a responsive physical world. Driving input visibly moves the vehicle. Apply immediate, legible cause and effect to our evidence selection.
- [Cosmograph](https://next.cosmograph.app/): inspected its public model atlas. The map occupies most of the canvas; selecting a point dims unrelated content and emphasizes connected paths. Apply meaningful graph emphasis and a quiet inspector. Never borrow its data or counts.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/): restrained type, hairline separation and a large actual product surface establish operational confidence. Apply consistent control states, calm report hierarchy and stable panels.

## Direction and anti-references

The intervention chamber: near-black machined surfaces, warm platinum, oxide red reserved for consequential requests, monumental Archivo display, Bodoni Moda italic contrast, Manrope operational text and Plex Mono identifiers. The mechanism and graph share hubs, struts and fractured connections.

Anti-references: generic dark SaaS card grids; neon cyber-security control rooms with fabricated metrics.

Five weaknesses in the previous render: thin wireframe material; weak typographic identity; undersized flat graph; unselectable connections; source messages detached from inspection.

Acceptance: complete cinematic with skip and reduced-motion fallback; actual local replay; keyboard-selectable graph nodes and links; exact source highlighting; specific protective action; original JSON export; preserved provider consent, cancellation, upload, redaction and scenarios; live desktop/mobile review with no horizontal overflow.

Graph depth is a deterministic spatial projection in SVG. Geometry encodes evidence type; position groups source, pattern and request. Depth conveys separation and can be rotated. It is not a probability, embedding similarity, or independent verification.

## Rendered review and iteration

Reviewed live in the app browser and through saved 1440×900 and 390×844 screenshots. `artifacts/rebuild-pass-1`, `artifacts/rebuild-pass-2` and `artifacts/final` preserve the visual evidence. The cinematic is also captured by the production browser suite in `test-results/cinematic-structure.png` and `test-results/home-webgl.png`.

Five most damaging findings in the first rebuilt render, followed by the corrections:

1. The hero's mobile fallback retained the old wireframe. Replaced it with a 35 KB render of the new physical mechanism, preserving the material and silhouette without loading WebGL.
2. Graph labels were too small and long names split awkwardly. Enlarged labels, wrapped them by words, reserved a separate classification line and fitted larger evidence records to the stage.
3. The graph had excessive vertical framing space and abrupt spatial rotation. Aligned the projection to the top, raised its content and added GSAP interpolation for nodes and curves. New links draw as their evidence arrives.
4. Selecting a payment connection exposed only the rule's tiny matched phrase and could leave the source low in the transcript. The inspector now shows the full exact message; the transcript highlights the matched phrase and scrolls only its own panel to reveal the source.
5. Completing analysis could leave the visitor below the result and change overall panel height. The desktop panels now reserve their dimensions and scroll internally; completion intentionally focuses and reveals the result. A delayed provider test checks unchanged panel width and height before, during and after completion.

Second-pass observations and refinements:

- Hero: reviewed typography, clear product statement, metallic reflections, original geometry and matching mobile art. The explanation and both actions remain usable during the opening.
- Cinematic: reviewed assembly, anomaly, fracture, camera passage, identity reveal and return to the hero. Added replay; verified skip, completion, session behavior and forced WebGL failure. Rendering stops when idle.
- Workspace: reviewed source, graph and action together. Existing uploads, redaction, consent, provider errors, cancellation, scenarios and canonical export remain functional.
- TrustGraph: reviewed both local and real provider results. Links are selectable with pointer or keyboard; connected evidence remains emphasized; all classifications and quotations retain their actual provenance. Larger records fit initially, with zoom and focus available for inspection.
- Replay: the actual engine determines the first escalation. Playback pauses there and selects a consequential connection. This gives the visitor time to read the triggering message and protective action. No step number or verdict is hardcoded.
- Mobile: re-authored the composition as transcript, pannable map and inspector. Checked 390 and 320 pixel widths, reduced motion, useful image rendering, controls and absence of page-level horizontal overflow.

The visual assessment is an implementation review, not a representative-user study or a claim to match the reference studios' production quality.
