# Research and implementation boundaries

Reviewed 16 October 2026. This is a focused design reference review, not a systematic literature review, benchmark evaluation or evidence of product efficacy.

| Primary source                                                                                                                                    | Observed scope                                                                                                                                                       | Design implication                                                                                                              | Implementation boundary                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sun et al., [PreScam: A Benchmark for Predicting Scam Progression from Early Conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12243), submitted May 2026 | A preprint benchmark studies progression and subsequent actions in conversational scams. Its abstract describes important limits in model progression understanding. | Inspect cumulative evidence across turns and make uncertainty visible.                                                          | CHAINBREAK does not implement its prediction tasks, use its dataset, reproduce results or predict a sender's next action. |
| Ai et al., [Defending Against Social Engineering Attacks in the Age of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12263), revised October 2024              | The work studies academic and recruitment conversations and proposes a modular defense pipeline operating at message and conversation levels.                        | Support both message-level review and the complete conversation; keep deterministic checks separate from contextual extraction. | CHAINBREAK does not implement ConvoSentinel, SEConvo retrieval or any reported performance.                               |
| FTC, [How To Avoid Scholarship and Financial Aid Scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-scholarship-and-financial-aid-scams)          | Consumer-protection guidance on scholarship promises, upfront fees, pressure and financial information requests.                                                     | Use explicit payment and information-request indicators in the fictional scholarship case.                                      | General guidance does not prove that any particular user-submitted exchange is fraudulent.                                |
| FTC, [Government Grant Scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/government-grant-scams)                                                           | Consumer-protection guidance about official-sounding identities and money or personal-information requests.                                                          | Separate claimed institutional identity from independent verification.                                                          | No institution or sender identity is independently checked by this product.                                               |

These implications are design decisions drawn from the sources, not claims that the sources validate our implementation. The source summaries are limited to the fetched primary-source abstracts or guidance. No participants were recruited and no user-study findings are invented.

## Privacy boundary

Local processing, optional redaction and avoiding application persistence reduce some exposure. They do not implement cryptographic access-pattern privacy. DuoRAM is not implemented and none of its guarantees are claimed. Model mode still transmits the selected text to the configured OpenAI service after consent.
